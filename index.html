• ABOUT THE GAME

Welcome to Three in a Line, a classic and engaging puzzle strategy game developed by Gaurav Tiwari. Challenge your mind, plan your moves, and try to align three of your marks in a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal row to win the game. It's a perfect brain exercise for players of all ages!

• HOW TO PLAY

  • • The game is played on a 3x3 grid.
  • • Players take turns placing their marks (X or O) on an empty square.
  • • The first player to get 3 of their marks in a row (up, down, across, or diagonally) is the winner.
  • • If all 9 squares are full and no player has 3 in a row, the game ends in a tie.
